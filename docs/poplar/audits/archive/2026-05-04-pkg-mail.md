## C8 — Structural symmetry

### types.go (file name) — C8 (T19)

`io` package precedent: `io.go`, `multi.go`, `pipe.go` — no `types.go`. Poplar's `types.go` is a catch-all by category (UID, Flag, Folder, UpdateType, ConnState, Update, Disposition, Attachment, ParseDisposition, ClassifyDisposition).

### changes.go houses ErrAuth + ErrNotFound — C8 (T19)

`ErrAuth` and `ErrNotFound` are Backend-level sentinels (drainer branches on them in response to Backend dispatch ops, not `ChangeTracker.Changes`). Only `ErrCannotCalculateChanges` belongs in `changes.go`. The grouping reflects "all errors in one place," not domain coupling.
